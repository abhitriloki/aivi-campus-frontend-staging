## 1. Vernacular AI Mock Interview Engine (Voice + Text Mode)

### 📌 Feature Description
An interactive, AI-powered interview practice room where candidates undergo realistic technical and HR mock interviews. Candidates can choose between **Voice Mode** (speaking into their microphone) and **Text Mode** (typing responses). The system supports **Hindi, Hinglish, and English** and evaluates answers in real time using a 0–10 scoring rubric across 4 dimensions: **Content, Fluency, Vocabulary, and Structure (STAR Method)**.

- **Voice Mode**: Powered by **Sarvam AI (Saaras v3 Speech-to-Text & Bulbul v3 Text-to-Speech)** and **Bhashini**, allowing natural speech interaction. It includes auto-playing AI question audio, live waveform recording animation, and a bilingual privacy notice.
- **Instant Detailed Feedback**: Provides score breakdowns, constructive bilingual feedback in English and Hindi, and an ideal model answer for every question.
- **Full Performance Scorecard**: Aggregates performance into an overall rating and assigns a Communication Band (*Basic, Conversational, Professional, Fluent*) with an actionable AI improvement roadmap.

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Most students in Tier-2/3 cities struggle with interview anxiety, poor English communication, or a lack of access to experienced mentors who can conduct practice interviews. Generic online mock tools only support strict English and lack real-time voice feedback.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Zero Language Barrier**: Students can practice in their natural language (Hinglish or Hindi) while gradually building confidence in technical English.
- **Realistic Practice**: Hearing the interviewer’s voice and speaking answers aloud simulates the pressure of actual hiring rounds.
- **Immediate Actionable Insights**: Shows students exactly why they scored low and provides model answers to memorize and learn from.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Unlike generic AI tools (like ChatGPT or basic video recorders) that only analyze text or demand expensive subscriptions, AIVI provides **native voice synthesis in Indian regional accents**, handles Hinglish technical terminology naturally, and grades according to actual Indian corporate hiring standards.

## 2. AI Resume Intelligence & ATS Optimization Engine

### 📌 Feature Description
A multi-layer AI resume scanner that evaluates a candidate’s resume against modern Applicant Tracking System (ATS) parsers and target job descriptions.

- **ATS Compatibility Score (0–100%)**: Analyzes formatting, section headers, bullet point impact, and keyword density.
- **Smart Keyword Matcher**: Identifies missing technical skills, tools, and industry keywords required for target job roles.
- **Action-Oriented Bullet Enhancer**: Converts weak resume lines (e.g. *"Worked on a React project"*) into high-impact, quantified achievements (e.g. *"Engineered a scalable React frontend, improving page load speed by 35% and serving 10k+ active users"*).

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Over 75% of resumes are automatically rejected by ATS software before a human recruiter ever sees them due to formatting errors, missing keywords, or weak phrasing.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Higher Interview Callback Rates**: Ensures resumes pass automated screening filters used by MNCs and startups.
- **Quantified Bullet Recommendations**: Teaches candidates how to frame their past projects with metrics and strong action verbs.
- **Instant Diagnostic**: Gives candidates a clear 0–100 score with step-by-step instructions on what to fix.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Most resume checkers only count generic keywords. AIVI uses deep contextual AI to evaluate bullet structure, technical accuracy, and domain relevance tailored specifically for freshers and early-career professionals.

## 3. Auto Sprint 7-Day & 14-Day Placement Preparation Plans

### 📌 Feature Description
An automated, personalized daily preparation curriculum generated whenever a student targets a specific company or role. 

- **Custom Daily Roadmaps**: Breaks down interview preparation into daily manageable tasks across 7 or 14 days (e.g. Day 1: Data Structures & Core Concepts, Day 2: System Design Basics, Day 3: Technical Mock Round).
- **Interactive Progress Checklists**: Allows students to check off completed topics, track daily streaks, and stay accountable.
- **Company-Specific Packs**: Tailors the daily curriculum based on target companies (e.g. TCS Ninja vs. Amazon SDE vs. Deloitte USI).

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Students often feel overwhelmed when a company placement drive is announced on short notice (often 7–10 days before the drive), leading to unstructured, last-minute cramming.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Structured Preparation**: Gives students a clear day-by-day roadmap so they know exactly what to study every morning.
- **Reduced Placement Stress**: Replaces panic with a structured, step-by-step preparation routine.
- **High Retention**: Small daily milestones ensure continuous learning without burnout.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Generic course platforms provide static, 6-month pre-recorded video playlists. AIVI Auto Sprint builds dynamic, time-boxed sprint tracks aligned specifically with the candidate's exact remaining days before an upcoming drive.

## 4. Real-Time Job Fit & Skills Gap Analyzer

### 📌 Feature Description

- **Match Score Percentage**: Gives an immediate compatibility rating (e.g. 78% Match).

- **Recommended Action Plan**: Suggests specific micro-courses, projects, or practice topics to bridge the gap before applying.

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Job seekers waste hundreds of hours applying to roles for which they are underqualified or missing core prerequisites, leading to high rejection rates and frustration.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Targeted Applications**: Helps candidates focus their energy only on roles where they have a high probability of success.
- **Clear Upskilling Blueprint**: Tells candidates exactly which 2–3 skills they need to learn to become a top candidate for their dream job.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Rather than giving vague feedback, AIVI provides precise skill-level matching with clear visual indicators, enabling instant application decisions.

## 10. High-Capacity Bulk CSV Student Onboarding

### 📌 Feature Description
An automated batch onboarding wizard that allows TPOs to import up to 5,000 student records at once via CSV.

- **Sample CSV Generator**: Provides a downloadable, pre-formatted CSV template (Name, Email, Roll Number, Branch, Batch Year, Phone).
- **Batch Validation Engine**: Validates email formatting, required fields, and checks for duplicates against existing tenant accounts.
- **Automated Passwordless Provisioning**: Creates official Firebase Auth accounts with custom tenant claims (`{tenant_id, role: "student"}`) and builds profile documents automatically.
- **Filterable Results & Failure CSV Export**: Shows a live progress bar, per-row success/skip/fail statuses, and allows the TPO to download a filtered "Failure Report CSV" to re-upload corrected rows.

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Manually creating hundreds or thousands of student accounts at the start of an academic year is slow, error-prone, and unsustainable for placement cells.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Onboard Entire Batches in Minutes**: Imports thousands of students in a single upload.
- **Clean Error Recovery**: The failure report CSV isolates bad rows so TPOs fix only errors without re-uploading valid students.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Provides real-time batch progress indicators and detailed error reporting instead of cryptic failure messages.

Extends drive creation with intelligent AI extraction, saving TPOs hours of manual data entry per drive.

## 13. Offer & Rejection Tracker + Package Privacy Engine

### 📌 Feature Description
A placement season outcome tracking module with built-in financial privacy controls.

- **Offer Details Entry**: When a candidate is moved to `Offered`, the system requires Package CTC (LPA), Offer Letter PDF upload, and Joining Date.
- **Rejection Reason Analytics**: When moved to `Rejected`, records structured rejection reasons (e.g. *Technical Round, System Design, Communication, HR Round*) to identify institutional training gaps.
- **Strict Package Privacy Controls**: Salary packages (CTC LPA) and offer letter URLs are visible **ONLY to authorized staff** (TPOs, Placement Directors, Super Admins). Students see only their own status (*"Offer Received — Congratulations!"*) and cannot view other students' salary numbers. Enforced at both database security rules and UI levels.
- **Automatic Placement Season Rollup**: Computes Highest Package, Average Package, Total Offers, and Branch-wise Placements.

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Prevents peer salary envy and privacy disputes among students while giving TPOs accurate placement analytics.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **DPDP & Privacy Compliance**: Protects student financial data from unauthorized exposure.
- **Actionable Training Insights**: Helps TPOs see why candidates are failing final rounds (e.g. 60% rejected in HR vs Technical).

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Enforces strict security rules at the database level rather than just hiding elements on the UI, eliminating inspect-element leaks.

## 14. Verified Resume ZIP Exporter + PDF Cover Sheet Generator

### 📌 Feature Description
A recruiter-facing exporter that bundles verified student resumes into a single organized ZIP package per placement drive.

- **Consent-Filtered Selection**: Includes only candidates who have engaged the drive (`Shortlisted` or `Offered` status). Unengaged/invited-only candidates are automatically excluded.
- **Automated PDF Cover Sheet**: Generates an institutional cover sheet PDF (`cover_sheet.pdf`) inside the ZIP featuring college logo, drive metadata, candidate summary, candidate index table, and confidentiality notices.
- **Candidate Index CSV**: Includes a `candidate_index.csv` for recruiter spreadsheet workflows.
- **7-Day Secure Signed URLs**: Uploads the ZIP to Firebase Storage and generates a 7-day expiring download link.
- **Large Pack Auto-Splitting**: Automatically splits large exports (>500 resumes) into branch-wise ZIP files.
- **Recruiter Email Delivery**: Emails the secure download link directly to the TPO and CCs the corporate recruiter via SMTP.

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
TPOs spend hours manually collecting, renaming, and emailing individual student PDF resumes to corporate recruiters.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Delights Corporate Recruiters**: Delivers a clean, branded ZIP with an index cover sheet and CSV index.
- **Time Saving**: Replaces hours of manual PDF gathering with a single click.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Produces recruiter-ready packages complete with a branded PDF cover sheet, CSV index, and automatic multi-branch splitting.

## 17. Campus AI Usage Metering, Budget Governance & Kill-Switch

### 📌 Feature Description
A cost-control and governance engine that tracks, limits, and manages AI token consumption and API costs across all campus tenants.

- **Real-Time Token & Call Metering**: Logs prompt tokens, completion tokens, estimated costs (INR/USD), and call counts daily and monthly per tenant.
- **Multi-Stage Budget Controls**:
  - **80% Warning Threshold**: Triggers an administrative warning event in audit logs.
  - **100% Hard Stop / Degradation**: When a tenant reaches 100% of their monthly token cap, AI features degrade to standard text/offline mode with a clear user notice instead of breaking.
- **Global Kill-Switch**: Allows platform admins to pause AI features system-wide during maintenance via environment configuration (`AI_FEATURES_OFF=true`).

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Uncontrolled AI API calls can lead to unexpected cloud bill spikes for institutions and platform operators.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Predictable Cloud Spend**: Guarantees AI API costs remain within monthly budget caps.
- **Graceful Service Degradation**: Prevents application crashes by switching to standard mode when caps are reached.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Includes built-in token-level metering and automated soft-degradation mechanisms.

## 18. Audit Logging & DPDP Act Data Privacy Compliance

### 📌 Feature Description
A platform-wide security, privacy, and compliance layer aligned with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act**.

- **Comprehensive Audit Trail**: Records administrative actions (role changes, tenant creation, resume verifications, drive outcome changes, SSO logins, data exports) in `audit_logs` with timestamps, actor UIDs, and IP metadata.
- **Strict In-Memory Processing**: Audio recordings during voice mock interviews are processed in-memory and discarded immediately; **zero raw audio is stored**.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Enforced via Firestore Security Rules, ensuring students access only their own data while restricting administrative data to verified staff.

### 🎯 What Problem It Solves
Ensures institutional compliance with data protection laws while maintaining an immutable audit trail for security audits.

### 🌟 Benefits
- **Legal Compliance**: Meets DPDP Act guidelines for student data handling.
- **Complete Accountability**: Tracks every administrative modification across campus portals.

### 🚀 How It Is Better Than Competitors
Combines database-enforced security rules with zero-audio-storage policies to deliver an enterprise-grade compliance foundation.
