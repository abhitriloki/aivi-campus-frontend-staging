# 3. Role 1: Training & Placement Officer (TPO) / Dean Manual

The Training & Placement Officer is the administrative commander of the campus. Below is the step-by-step guide for every screen and every button available to the TPO.

### 3.1 Setting Up Institutional Identity & Branding
* **Where to find it:** Left Sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Operations** $\rightarrow$ Click **"Tenant Settings"** (`/campus/settings`).

#### What this screen does:
Transforms generic software into the college’s own official branded portal. All students and recruiters will see the college's official name, emblem, and official colors.

#### Button-by-Button and Field Guide:
1. **"Institution Display Name" (Text Field):**
   * *What to enter:* The official statutory name of your college (e.g., `National Institute of Technology, Trichy` or `Sunrise College of Engineering & Technology`).
   * *What it does:* Appears on the top header, student login screen, and on official generated PDF reports.
2. **"Institutional Code / Slug" (Read-Only / Fixed):**
   * *What it displays:* The unique web address identifier for your college (e.g., `nitt` or `sunrise`).
   * *What it does:* Forms your dedicated login address: `https://aivicampus.com/campus/login/<your-slug>`.
3. **"Upload Institutional Logo" (Upload Button):**
   * *What it does:* Click to upload your college seal or crest (PNG, JPG, or SVG). 
   * *Where it appears:* Embedded on the student login screen, top sidebar, and stamped on the header of all official NAAC audit PDFs.
4. **"Primary Theme Color" (Color Picker & Hex Box):**
   * *What to select:* Choose your college’s official primary color (e.g., Navy Blue `#0A2540`, Maroon `#800000`, Forest Green `#143E3B`).
   * *What it does:* Instantly changes the color of all primary buttons, navigation headers, and progress bars across the entire student and staff interface.
5. **"Accent Highlight Color" (Color Picker & Hex Box):**
   * *What to select:* Choose a complementary accent color (e.g., Gold `#D59C45`, Cyan `#20CFC0`).
   * *What it does:* Sets the color of active badges, score rings, and notification tags.
6. **"Academic Year" (Dropdown):**
   * *Options:* `2024-25`, `2025-26`, `2026-27`.
   * *What it does:* Anchors all placement drives and reports to the currently active graduating batch.
7. **"Active Branches & Departments" (Tags / Input):**
   * *What to enter:* Comma-separated list of departments (e.g., `CSE, IT, ECE, ME, CE, MBA`).
   * *What it does:* Automatically creates the filter categories used in Student Directory, Placement Drives, and NAAC tables.
8. **"Save Institutional Settings" (Primary Action Button):**
   * *What it does:* Saves all configurations to the central cloud. The portal immediately reflects the college branding without needing any page reload.

### 3.2 Onboarding Students via Bulk CSV Import
* **Where to find it:** Left Sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Readiness** $\rightarrow$ Click **"Students Roster"** $\rightarrow$ Click **"Import Roster"** (`/campus/import`).

#### What this screen does:
Eliminates manual student registration. The TPO can onboard an entire batch of 500, 1,000, or 5,000 students in less than 2 minutes using an ordinary Excel or CSV file.

#### Step-by-Step Button & Action Guide:
1. **"Download Sample CSV Template" (Secondary Button):**
   * *What it does:* Downloads a ready-to-use CSV template with pre-formatted headers: `Full Name`, `Email`, `Enrollment No`, `Branch`, `CGPA`, `Academic Year`, `Phone`.
2. **"Drop CSV File Here or Click to Browse" (Upload Dropzone):**
   * *What to do:* Drag and drop your college’s student master list from your computer.
3. **"Column Mapping Wizard" (Dropdown Selectors):**
   * *What it does:* Matches the columns from your Excel file to AIVI’s database:
     * *First Name / Full Name* $\rightarrow$ Select your file's Name column.
     * *Email ID* $\rightarrow$ Select your file's Email column.
     * *Enrollment / Roll Number* $\rightarrow$ Select your file's Roll No column.
     * *Branch / Department* $\rightarrow$ Select your file's Department column.
     * *CGPA* $\rightarrow$ Select your file's CGPA column.
4. **"Validate Roster Records" (Action Button):**
   * *What it does:* Pre-scans every row in your file. It flags errors immediately (e.g., missing email, duplicate roll number, or invalid CGPA format) without breaking the import.
5. **"Execute Student Import & Account Provisioning" (Green Action Button):**
   * *What happens when clicked:*
     * Automatically creates secure accounts for every student.
     * Generates their private profile in the college database.
     * Pre-populates their branch and CGPA so students cannot artificially inflate their marks.
     * Dispatches welcome activation instructions to their email addresses.

### 3.3 Managing the Student Directory & Employability Scores
* **Where to find it:** Left Sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Readiness** $\rightarrow$ Click **"Students Roster"** (`/campus/students`).

#### What this screen does:
Serves as the central master registry of every enrolled candidate. It provides live visibility into who is placement-ready, who has applied to drives, and who is falling behind.

#### Button-by-Button Guide:
1. **"Search Candidates" (Search Input Box):**
   * *What to do:* Type any student's name, email, or enrollment number to locate their record in 100 milliseconds.
2. **"Branch Filter" (Dropdown):**
   * *Options:* `All Branches`, `CSE`, `ECE`, `ME`, `CE`, `MBA`, etc.
   * *What it does:* Filters the table to show candidates from specific departments only.
3. **"AES Score Filter" (Dropdown):**
   * *What AES is:* **AIVI Employability Score (0 to 100)** — an objective, AI-calculated composite score combining resume ATS compatibility, technical skills, project depth, and mock interview performance.
   * *Options:* `All Scores`, `High Readiness (80-100)`, `Moderate (60-79)`, `Needs Attention (<60)`.
4. **"Placement Status Filter" (Dropdown):**
   * *Options:* `All Candidates`, `Unplaced`, `Applied to Drive`, `Shortlisted`, `Offered / Placed`.
5. **"View Student Dossier" (Eye Icon on any row):**
   * *What it does:* Opens a complete student scorecard showing:
     * Full contact details and academic history.
     * Their verified ATS resume with download link.
     * Skill Radar (technical keywords matched vs missing).
     * Voice Interview report with filler-word metrics and speaking pace.
6. **"Export Filtered Directory (CSV)" (Secondary Action Button):**
   * *What it does:* Downloads an Excel spreadsheet of the currently filtered view, perfect for department meetings or recruiter inquiries.

### 3.4 Creating & Running Placement Drives with AI
* **Where to find it:** Left Sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Readiness** $\rightarrow$ Click **"Placement Drives"** (`/campus/drives`).

#### What this screen does:
This is where the TPO organizes campus recruitment drives for visiting companies (e.g., Google, TCS, Infosys, Amazon, L&T). It automates job description analysis and student eligibility checks.

#### Button-by-Button Guide to Creating a Drive:
1. **"+ Create New Placement Drive" (Primary Top-Right Button):**
   * *What it does:* Opens the 3-step Placement Drive Creator wizard.
2. **"Company Name" (Text Field):**
   * *What to enter:* e.g., `Microsoft India` or `Tata Consultancy Services`.
3. **"Role Title" (Text Field):**
   * *What to enter:* e.g., `Software Development Engineer - 1` or `Graduate Engineer Trainee`.
4. **"Paste Corporate Job Description (JD)" (Large Text Area):**
   * *What to do:* Paste the raw job specification received from the company HR or recruiter.
5. **"Parse JD with AI" (Sparkles Button):**
   * *What happens when clicked:* AIVI’s integrated Gemini 2.5 Flash artificial intelligence reads the entire JD in 2 seconds and automatically extracts:
     * *Required Technical Skills* (e.g., React, Java, SQL, AWS).
     * *Minimum CGPA Requirement* (e.g., 7.5 CGPA).
     * *Recommended Branches* (e.g., CSE, IT, ECE).
     * *Interview Rounds* (e.g., Aptitude $\rightarrow$ Technical $\rightarrow$ Managerial $\rightarrow$ HR).
6. **"Minimum CGPA Threshold" (Number Input):**
   * *What to do:* Review or adjust the minimum CGPA required to apply (e.g., `7.0`). Students below this number will be blocked from applying.
7. **"Eligible Branches" (Multi-Select Checkboxes):**
   * *What to do:* Check `CSE`, `ECE`, etc., or check `Allow All Branches`.
8. **"Offered Package / CTC" (Text Input):**
   * *What to enter:* e.g., `₹12.5 LPA` or `₹4.5 - 6.0 LPA`.
9. **"Application Deadline & Drive Date" (Date Pickers):**
   * *What to do:* Set the last date for student submissions and the scheduled campus interview date.
10. **"Publish Placement Drive" (Green Action Button):**
    * *What happens immediately:*
      * The drive goes live instantly.
      * It appears automatically on the dashboards of all eligible students.
      * Ineligible students (e.g., Mechanical students if only CSE is selected, or students below 7.0 CGPA) will see why they are not eligible, preventing manual arguments at the placement office.

### 3.5 Managing Candidate Pipelines (Shortlisting to Offers)
* **Where to find it:** Inside any published drive on `/campus/drives`.

#### What this screen does:
Provides a visual recruitment pipeline (Kanban Board & Table) tracking every applicant from initial application to final job offer.

#### Button-by-Button Guide:
1. **"Run AI Shortlist" (Primary Sparkles Button):**
   * *What happens when clicked:* The system cross-references every student who applied against the company’s requirements:
     * Checks academic eligibility (CGPA and branch).
     * Verifies that the student has a **TPO-Verified Resume** with an active QR code.
     * Computes the semantic match score between the student’s skills and the company’s JD.
     * Automatically tags candidates as `Eligible & Shortlisted` or flags specific ineligibility reasons (e.g., *Missing DPDP Consent* or *CGPA 6.8 < 7.0*).
2. **"View Mode: Table vs Kanban" (Toggle Buttons):**
   * *Table View:* Comprehensive list of applicants with sortable columns (CGPA, AES Score, Branch, Stage).
   * *Kanban View:* Visual drag-and-drop columns:
     `Applied` $\rightarrow$ `Shortlisted` $\rightarrow$ `Technical Evaluation` $\rightarrow$ `Interview Scheduled` $\rightarrow$ `Offered / Selected`.
3. **"Advance Stage" (Arrow Button on Candidate Row):**
   * *What it does:* Moves a student who cleared a test or round to the next interview stage.
4. **"Record Offer" (Trophy Icon Button):**
   * *What it does:* Opens the Official Placement Record Modal:
     * *Confirmed Package (LPA)*: e.g., `14.5`.
     * *Offer Letter Reference / URL*: Link or file ID for the offer letter.
     * *Tentative Joining Date*: e.g., `2026-07-01`.
   * *What happens when saved:*
     * Student's status is permanently updated to **"Placed"**.
     * The college’s Placement Rate and Average CTC metrics update immediately.
     * The offer is permanently logged for **NAAC Metric 5.2.1** and **NIRF** reporting.
5. **"Record Rejection" (X Icon Button):**
   * *What it does:* Opens the Rejection Feedback Modal with structured reasons:
     * *Did not clear Aptitude Test*
     * *Did not clear Technical Round*
     * *Did not clear HR Round*
     * *Candidate Declined Offer*
   * *Why this is critical:* The system uses this feedback to recommend targeted preparation sprints for that student so they clear their next drive.

### 3.6 The Verified Resume Vault & One-Click Recruiter ZIP Exporter
* **Where to find it:** Left Sidebar $\rightarrow$ **Readiness** $\rightarrow$ Click **"Resume Vault"** (`/campus/vault`).

#### What this screen does:
Solves the #1 complaint of corporate recruiters: receiving messy, mismatched, unverified student resumes. It allows the college to verify resumes and export an entire batch in a single click.

#### Button-by-Button Guide:
1. **"Review Resume" (Eye Icon on any student row):**
   * *What it does:* Opens the full PDF viewer alongside the student's ATS Scorecard.
2. **"Approve & Stamp QR Code" (Green Shield Button):**
   * *What happens when clicked:* 
     * The system stamps an official, tamper-proof **Institutional Verification Seal** and a **Dynamic QR Code** onto the student's PDF resume.

     * Click **"Dispatch Official Package"**.
     * The system’s background engine packages all 45 verified, QR-stamped PDF resumes into a clean, standardized ZIP archive and emails a secure download link directly to the corporate recruiter via official institutional mail.

# 4. Role 2: Department Professors & Faculty Placement Coordinators Manual

In modern universities, the central TPO cannot oversee thousands of students alone. Department HODs and Faculty Placement Coordinators (e.g., CSE Placement Coordinator, Mechanical Coordinator) play a vital role.

---

### 4.1 How Faculty Are Invited & Scoped to Branches
* **How it happens:** The TPO navigates to **Operations** $\rightarrow$ **"Staff & Roles"** (`/campus/staff`).
* The TPO clicks **"+ Invite Faculty Coordinator"**, enters the professor’s name and official email, and selects their **Branch Scope** (e.g., `["CSE"]` or `["ECE"]`).
* The professor receives an invitation link to log in at `https://aivicampus.com/campus/login/<slug>`.

---

### 4.2 Departmental Dashboard & Student Readiness Tracking
When a professor logs in:
1. **Department Isolation:** The professor does not get overwhelmed by the entire university’s data. If Prof. Sharma is the CSE Coordinator, his dashboard automatically filters to show **only CSE students** and **CSE-eligible placement drives**.
2. **Tracking Weak Students:** The professor can click on **"Students Roster"** and filter by `AES < 60`. This gives the department an instant list of students who need remedial technical classes or resume coaching.

---

### 4.3 Reviewing Student Resumes & Leaving Project Feedback
Faculty members are best equipped to verify whether a student’s technical projects are genuine.
1. The professor opens **"Resume Vault"** (`/campus/vault`).
2. The table shows submitted resumes from their department.
3. The professor clicks **"Review Resume"** to inspect the student’s listed capstone projects, internships, and technical skills.
4. If a student claims an unrealistic project or has poor descriptions, the professor clicks **"Request Changes"** and leaves direct academic advice (e.g., *"Explain your database indexing technique in Project 2"*).
5. Once satisfied, the professor clicks **"Approve & Stamp QR Code"**.

---

### 4.4 Tracking Departmental Drive Applications & Offers
1. The professor can open **"Placement Drives"** (`/campus/drives`) to see which companies are visiting.
2. Under any drive (e.g., *Microsoft SDE*), the professor can view exactly which students from their department applied, who cleared the coding round, and who reached the final interviews.
3. If an offer is received, the coordinator can assist the TPO by clicking **"Record Offer"** and attaching the student's confirmation.

---

### 4.5 Supporting Departmental NBA / NAAC Criteria Preparation
During National Board of Accreditation (NBA) audits for specific engineering departments:
1. The HOD or Coordinator navigates to **"NAAC / NIRF Evidence"** (`/campus/accreditation`).
2. They select their department filter.
3. The system generates branch-specific placement records, higher education progression counts, and average salaries required for **NBA Criterion 5 (Student Performance)** without requiring faculty to spend weekends sorting paper files.

### 5.1 Logging In & Data Privacy Consent (DPDP Act 2023)

# 7. Layman's Glossary & Frequently Asked Questions

### 📘 Plain-English Glossary:
* **ATS (Applicant Tracking System):** Automated software used by companies (like Google, TCS, or Deloitte) to scan incoming resumes. If a resume has tables, graphics, or lacks keywords, the ATS rejects it before a human ever sees it.
* **AES (AIVI Employability Score):** A composite score from 0 to 100 calculated by AIVI measuring a student's true placement readiness, combining resume quality, technical skills, and verbal interview performance.
* **QR Verification Seal:** A cryptographic square barcode stamped on an approved resume. When scanned by a recruiter's smartphone, it opens an official college page confirming that the candidate's marks, degree, and projects have been verified by the university.
* **STAR Framework:** A globally recognized interview methodology standing for **Situation, Task, Action, and Result**. Top corporate recruiters score answers based on this structure.
* **NAAC Criterion 5:** The National Assessment and Accreditation Council's standard governing **"Student Support and Progression"**, specifically tracking how many students receive training and secure employment.
* **NIRF Metric 3.a:** The National Institutional Ranking Framework's metric measuring student placement percentages and median annual salaries.
* **DPDP Act 2023:** India's statutory Digital Personal Data Protection Act ensuring that student personal information and voice recordings remain completely private and sovereign.

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

#### Q1: Can a student fake their CGPA to apply for a high-paying drive?
**No.** When the TPO imports the student roster via CSV, the student's CGPA and branch are locked to official college records. A student cannot artificially change their marks to bypass eligibility criteria.

#### Q2: What happens if a recruiter receives a resume from an AIVI campus?
The recruiter receives a clean, standardized, single-column ATS-compliant PDF. At the top-right corner is the college's official crest and a tamper-proof verification QR code. Scanning the QR code instantly validates that the resume is authentic, eliminating fraudulent applications.

#### Q3: Does the AI Voice Interview save student audio on external servers?
**No.** Audio processing occurs in live memory via the Web Audio API and Speech Recognition. Audio streams are never stored or sold to third parties, ensuring strict compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023.

#### Q4: How long does it take for a newly signed college to go live?
**Less than 60 minutes.** Because AIVI Campus OS is a zero-downtime multi-tenant cloud platform, there is no need to deploy servers or install local software. Once the TPO enters the college details and uploads the student CSV, students and faculty can log in immediately.

#### Q5: Can departmental professors see students from other departments?
**By default, no.** Faculty Placement Coordinators are scoped to their assigned departments (e.g., Prof. Verma sees only CSE students). The central TPO Director and College Dean retain global visibility across all university branches.

#### Q6: Can a college customize the portal with its own domain or colors?
**Yes.** Under **Tenant Settings**, the college can set its official logo, brand colors, and academic year. The system automatically creates a dedicated portal URL (e.g., `https://aivicampus.com/campus/login/nit-trichy`).
