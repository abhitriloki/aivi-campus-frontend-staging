# AIVI Brand Guidelines

This document defines the official brand system for **AIVI Career Intelligence OS**.

It should be treated as the visual source of truth for:

- marketing pages
- dashboard and product pages
- navigation
- feature modules
- documentation
- investor materials
- social and brand assets

The purpose of this guideline is to ensure that every future page, component, and surface feels consistent and aligned with the same product identity.

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

### Brand Name

**AIVI**

### Expanded Positioning

**AIVI Career Intelligence OS**

### What AIVI Is

AIVI is a premium career intelligence platform that helps users:

- analyze their resume
- measure job fit
- identify skill gaps
- improve positioning
- rebuild resumes strategically
- prepare for interviews
- grow toward better roles

### What AIVI Is Not

AIVI should not be positioned visually or verbally as:

- a generic AI tool
- a chatbot wrapper
- a basic resume generator
- a flashy “future AI” startup template
- an internal ATS or HR admin panel

### Core Brand Idea

**AIVI is the strategic operating system for your career.**

---

## 2. Brand Personality

The AIVI brand should consistently feel:

- strategic
- premium
- intelligent
- trustworthy
- structured
- calm
- modern
- precise
- high-agency

The brand should avoid feeling:

- playful
- noisy
- gimmicky
- neon-techy
- over-automated
- generic SaaS-template driven

---

## 3. Visual Direction

The AIVI visual system should sit between:

- editorial elegance
- premium analytics product
- modern career strategy platform

### Design Qualities

- high contrast hierarchy
- refined spacing
- controlled use of accent color
- premium typography
- layered but calm surfaces
- elegant metric presentation
- rich but not flashy interaction language

### Avoid

- excessive glow
- purple-heavy palettes
- hyper-saturated gradients
- overcrowded cards
- generic “AI dark mode” visuals
- flat, under-designed minimalism

---

## 4. Logo System

### Approved Direction

The approved AIVI logo direction is:

**Strategic Monogram**

### Concept Meaning

The logo is a refined, geometric `A` mark with a subtle upward intelligence path embedded inside it.

It represents:

- upward career progression
- structured guidance
- intelligence-led growth
- strategic advancement

### Logo Asset Folder

Official logo assets live in:

`public/brand/`

### Current Approved Asset Set

- `aivi-mark.svg`
- `aivi-logo-dark.svg`
- `aivi-logo-light.svg`
- `aivi-logo-horizontal.svg`
- `aivi-favicon.svg`
- `aivi-app-icon.svg`

### Logo Usage Principles

- Use the **symbol + wordmark** for top-level brand moments
- Use the **symbol only** in constrained spaces such as sidebar, favicon, badges, and app icons
- Prefer monochrome or restrained-accent usage over decorative gradients
- Do not distort, stretch, rotate, or apply heavy effects to the logo

---

## 5. Color System

The AIVI palette should feel premium, intelligent, and calm.

### Primary Colors

- **Deep Ink**
  - Primary dark surface / premium background
  - Suggested direction: `#0F1720`

- **Graphite**
  - Secondary dark surface / panels / structure
  - Suggested direction: `#1E2933`

- **Warm Ivory**
  - Light background / premium content contrast
  - Suggested direction: `#F5F0E8`

- **Muted Stone**
  - Neutral supporting surface
  - Suggested direction: `#D9D1C7`

### Accent Colors

- **Deep Teal**
  - Primary action accent
  - Suggested direction: `#1C6B68`

- **Soft Teal**
  - Supporting highlight / secondary accent
  - Suggested direction: `#2D8B86`

- **Muted Gold**
  - Selective premium detail / insight emphasis
  - Suggested direction: `#B5965A`

### Functional Colors

- Success: refined green, not neon
- Warning: warm amber, not bright yellow
- Error: muted red, not over-saturated
- Info: slate-blue or cool teal depending on surface

### Color Usage Principles

- Deep teal should be the main interactive accent
- Gold should be used sparingly for premium details
- Neutrals should do most of the heavy visual work
- The palette should remain elegant and restrained

---

## 6. Typography Direction

Typography is central to AIVI’s premium feel.

### Brand Typographic Strategy

Use a two-part system:

- a refined, high-credibility display/headline face
- a clean, modern sans for product UI and body copy

### Desired Typographic Feel

- serious
- polished
- editorial
- readable
- stable
- intentional

### Headline Usage

Headlines should:

- feel strong and premium
- have clear visual rhythm
- avoid overly casual or trendy expression

### Body Usage

Body text should:

- remain crisp and readable
- support dense product explanation
- work well in dashboard and marketing contexts

### Metrics / Numbers

Numbers should be displayed in a clean, premium style with strong alignment and clarity.

---

## 7. Layout and Spacing Principles

All AIVI layouts should feel intentional and structured.

### Layout Rules

- use clear grid-based composition
- maintain visual breathing room
- avoid crowding content into card walls
- create hierarchy with spacing before decoration

### Section Rhythm

Sections should feel:

- layered
- purposeful
- visually distinct
- easy to scan

### Product Layout Principle

Insight should always lead to action.

Every major product section should make it obvious:

- what the user is seeing
- why it matters
- what to do next

---

## 8. Component Style Guidelines

### Buttons

Buttons should feel:

- premium
- high-contrast
- modern
- substantial but not bulky

Primary buttons:

- deep teal / premium accent
- strong contrast
- clear hover treatment

Secondary buttons:

- refined outline or muted surface
- subtle but high-quality

### Cards

Cards should:

- have depth
- not feel generic
- avoid random equal weight everywhere
- support information hierarchy

### Forms

Forms should feel:

- premium
- calm
- easy to use
- highly readable

Textareas and upload surfaces should be visibly better than default SaaS inputs.

### Charts and Metrics

Charts should:

- feel analytical
- stay readable
- avoid over-decoration
- use clean accent signals

---

## 9. Motion Guidelines

Motion should be present, but controlled.

### Motion Personality

- calm
- confident
- layered
- strategic

### Appropriate Motion Use

- hero entrance
- staggered feature reveal
- panel transitions
- dashboard stat loading
- hover refinement
- modal and sheet transitions

### Avoid

- playful bounce
- over-animated glowing effects
- constant movement
- distracting ambient motion

---

## 10. Imagery and Illustration

Images should support a premium, strategic product narrative.

### Recommended Image Direction

- sophisticated UI mockups
- outcome-driven visuals
- subtle texture and environment
- premium product screenshots
- structured insight cards

### Avoid

- cliché stock office imagery
- playful AI illustrations
- generic tech abstract glows
- cartoonish productivity visuals

---

## 11. Voice and Copy Style

The AIVI brand voice should feel:

- intelligent
- calm
- persuasive
- precise
- credible

### Copy Principles

- be clear, not vague
- be premium, not inflated
- sound strategic, not robotic
- focus on outcomes, not buzzwords

### Example Brand Tone

Good:

- “The strategic operating system for your career”
- “Measure fit, identify gaps, and improve with precision”
- “Turn career uncertainty into actionable intelligence”

Avoid:

- “AI magic”
- “revolutionary chatbot”
- “supercharge your future with next-gen intelligence”

## 15. Implementation Rules for Future Design Work

All future redesign work should follow this sequence:

1. check this guideline
2. use approved logo assets
3. use approved palette direction
4. preserve tone and hierarchy principles
5. extend from the same component language

No major page or module should introduce:

- unrelated colors
- random typography
- unrelated motion language
- a conflicting product tone
